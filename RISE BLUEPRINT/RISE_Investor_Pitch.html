The Simple Truth

Every morning is a fresh start.
Most people waste it.

There are over 2 billion smartphones on Earth. Almost every single one has an alarm clock on it. People set their alarm, it rings, they hit snooze — and repeat this same cycle for years.

Nothing about that alarm clock is on their side.

It does not care if they actually got up. It does not remember that they have been hitting snooze for 400 days straight. It does not know that they promised themselves this year would be different. It just rings. They just snooze. And the morning — the most powerful part of the day — disappears.

RISE is an alarm app that does not let you go back to sleep — and then helps you understand why you keep trying to.

The Product

What RISE actually does

RISE is built around one core idea: waking up is not a single moment, it is a habit — and habits are built through accountability, identity, and understanding.

Layer 1 — The Alarm That Holds You Accountable

When your RISE alarm fires, you cannot dismiss it by pressing a button. You have to prove you are awake:

  • Complete a wake game (mathematics, memory, word puzzles) — difficulty set by you
  • Optional: place your finger on the camera for a heartbeat reading
  • 15 minutes later, a follow-up check confirms you did not go back to sleep
  • If you do not confirm, your accountability buddy receives an automatic message

Layer 2 — The Life Tracking That Keeps You Going

RISE quietly tracks the things that affect your mornings:

  • How long and how well you slept
  • What you ate and when — with full support for African, South Asian, Middle Eastern and global cuisines, not just Western foods
  • Your daily intentions and morning tasks
  • Your habit streak and consistency over time

Layer 3 — The AI That Explains You To Yourself

This is what makes RISE genuinely different:

Every week, RISE writes you a personal review. Not a list of numbers and graphs — a real paragraph, in plain language, by an AI that has been watching your patterns. It tells you what it noticed, what the data suggests, and what to carry forward.

Every month, it writes you a letter. Addressed to you by name. About who you were this month based on what you actually did — not who you planned to be.

This is the product people pay for. Everything else — the alarm, the streak, the games, the food tracker, the quotes, the goal journal — is free. Forever.

The Conversion Mechanic That Actually Works

Free users who have been using RISE for a week see the first paragraph of their weekly review in full. The rest is blurred out. One button: "See your full review."

This is not a generic sales pitch. It is their own data. Their own story. The first paragraph was already compelling enough to make them want the rest. Our projected conversion rate from this mechanic alone: 8–12% of free users who see it.

Advertising — The Secondary Revenue

One sponsor message per week, shown at the bottom of the weekly review screen, free users only, clearly labeled "Sponsored." Never in the morning ritual. Never intrusive.

We have intentionally left this revenue stream dark until the user base is large enough to approach brands directly. When we have 200,000+ active free users, we will offer a single, relevant sponsor placement at a flat monthly rate — clean, editorial, and worth something to the right brand.

A Feature Worth Highlighting

The Guardian Plan

One of the most underserved markets in the wellness app space is parents who want their children to actually get up for school. RISE's Guardian plan speaks directly to this:

  • A parent sets the alarm. The child cannot turn it off, change the difficulty, or delete it.
  • The child must complete a wake game to dismiss it. The parent is notified when the alarm fires and when it is dismissed.
  • If the child does not dismiss it within a set window, the parent receives an escalating alert.
  • The parent can see the child's wake game results over time.

At $6.99–$16.99 per month, this is the highest-value plan in the lineup. Parents pay for peace of mind. The product genuinely delivers it.

This segment is largely untouched by every competitor in the market. It is a clear, paying, motivated audience with a real problem that RISE solves completely.
